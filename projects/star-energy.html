01 / PROJECT CONTEXT

Environmental spatial information for operational-area management and community initiatives.

The internship involved the Safety, Health and Environment (SHE) and Policy, Government and Public Affairs (PGPA) departments. The work supported environmental management and community-related initiatives through spatial information, field observations, and mapping outputs.
02 / PROJECT 01 — GREEN OPEN SPACE

Inventory, classify, and prioritize green open spaces for field verification.

  • Digitized and inventoried 93 Green Open Space (RTH) polygons covering approximately 79.7 hectares.
  • Used Google Earth Pro, ArcGIS, QGIS, Avenza Maps, and field observation for spatial digitization and checking.
  • Processed Landsat 8 imagery in Google Earth Engine to derive NDVI and Land Surface Temperature (LST).
  • Applied a 50:50 weighted overlay to prioritize vegetation-management areas.
  • Checked 15 priority points in the field, recording 80% agreement with observed field conditions.
03 / PROJECT 02 — CIPANAS FLOOD RISK

A spatial risk map to support community ecotourism planning.

The flood-risk model integrated elevation, slope, distance to river, rainfall, land use/land cover, and soil type. Parameter weights were tested and adjusted iteratively, informed by interviews with nearby residents about historical flood frequency. The work included collaborative discussion, a hybrid presentation, and field validation to refine the spatial representation of flood-prone areas.
04 / MY ROLE

Supporting staff contribution across GIS processing, field observation, and project communication.

My contribution included spatial digitization, remote-sensing analysis, GIS visualization, field observation, priority-point checking, and participation in discussions and presentations. The internship outputs were developed in a team and departmental context; the project results are not presented as solely individual deliverables.
05 / OUTPUTS

Environmental maps and spatial references for operational and community contexts.

Outputs included a green open-space inventory map, vegetation and thermal analysis maps, a priority field-check map, a Cipanas river-tubing flood-risk map, and ecotourism site-planning visuals. The mapped 80% field agreement refers specifically to the 15 priority points checked; it is not a model accuracy statistic.
