01 / BUSINESS QUESTION

How can transaction-level data become a reliable operating dashboard?

The case uses a simulated Korean restaurant dataset for the 2025 calendar year. The analysis focuses on completed orders, menu/category performance, order type, time patterns, and the reliability of automated reporting. The dataset contains item-level transaction rows, so rows, unique orders, servings, and revenue are treated as different measures.
02 / DATA & WORKFLOW

From raw transaction rows to an interactive one-page dashboard.

  • Data scope: 20,000 item-detail rows, 7,184 unique order IDs, 102 unique menu items, and 10 categories.
  • Data preparation: data validation, TRIM, lookup checks, and conditional formulas.
  • Analysis: COUNTIF, COUNTIFS, SUMIFS, AVERAGEIFS, VLOOKUP, IFERROR, and PivotTables.
  • Dashboard: three KPIs, PivotCharts, and slicers for day type and category.
  • Automation: VBA macro for formatting, formulas, PivotTable refresh, and PowerPoint report generation.
03 / PERFORMANCE FINDINGS

Revenue concentration, operating patterns, and margin differences.

  • Completed sales totalled Rp1,576,355,800 across 6,831 completed orders and 25,328 servings.
  • Gross profit was Rp918,319,800, equivalent to a 58.3% gross margin.
  • Dine In contributed 64% of completed sales.
  • BBQ / Grill and Paket / Set Menu together contributed 52.0% of sales, while their gross margins (54.2% and 49.7%) were below the overall 58.3% margin.
  • Minuman and Dessert had higher gross margins (75.1% and 71.3%) but smaller sales contributions, suggesting an upsell test worth evaluating.
04 / DATA QUALITY & DEBUGGING

A working macro is not proof that the output is correct.

During VBA validation, the initial macro summed the subtotal-before-discount column instead of the final total column. The discrepancy was Rp692,200. The reference was corrected to the appropriate total column and reconciled against the source workbook. The analysis also identified 118 identical rows; they were not automatically deleted because the source lacked a unique item-row identifier.
05 / MY CONTRIBUTION & LIMITATIONS

Analysis, dashboard development, validation, and reporting.

I prepared the dataset, built formula-driven analysis, developed PivotTable summaries and dashboard visualizations, implemented basic VBA automation, and checked the resulting totals against the source workbook. The dataset is simulated for training; results should not be interpreted as actual restaurant performance or client work.
